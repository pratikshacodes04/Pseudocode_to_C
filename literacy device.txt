• ALLITERATION-when a same "alphabet" is repeated in two consecutive words.
• REPETITION- when a same "word" is repeated consecutively in "a" line 
• ANAPHORA-when a same word is repeated in first line of two consecutive(ek ke baad ek)lines .
• METAPHOR- when two objects are compared together without using 'like' or' as' Eg Dearsir is god of english😎.
• SIMILE- when two objects are compared together with use of words like 'like' and 'as'. Eg she look like moon etc😅.
• ONOMATOPOEIA- sounds like buzz,hiss,thud etc
• ALLUSION- situation compared to cartoon or historical characters.like Rapunzel etc.
• ASSONANCE- repetition of vowels in consecutive words.
• CONSONANCE-repetition of consonant sound in consecutive words 
• OXYMORON- contrasting words in a single word like bittersweet and quiterage.
• SYMBOLISM- any object symoblysis any other thing.
• IMAGERY-an image of event is formed by the poet's text.
• IRONY- contrasting situations 
ENJAMBMENT- no use of punctuations in stanza.
• PERSONIFICATION-when any animal or non living thing is given human properties. Eg Sun burry its feets.
